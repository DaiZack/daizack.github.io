Selected Work

Matching 250 million companies, so underwriters decide on the right entity

COFACE · SENIOR DATA SCIENTIST · 2023 — NOW · PYTHON · ELASTICSEARCH · BERT · DUCKDB · KUBERNETES

Problem
The same company appears under dozens of names, addresses and IDs across 50+ sources. Match it wrong, and a credit decision is made on the wrong company.
Method
A hybrid pipeline I designed and own: staged retrieval first, learned scoring second, rules last — never the other way round.
Result
Index-to-index matching built from scratch across global sources. The U.S. full-index match went from 27 days to 6 days after I rebuilt it with producer–worker distributed scheduling.
Oracle export→Normalize→Elasticsearch→Exact, then fuzzy retrieval→BERT name + address scoring→Rule ranking→API & batch

Productionized with RabbitMQ, Kubernetes and GitLab CI/CD — automated deployment, monitoring, scaling, real-time error correction, and a human in the loop where the machine is unsure.
