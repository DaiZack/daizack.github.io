Spotlight · Case Study · Coface

Matching 250M companies, so underwriters decide on the right entity

● In productionProprietary — case study only

Problem: the same company appears under dozens of names, addresses and IDs across 50+ sources; match it wrong and a credit decision lands on the wrong company. Method: staged retrieval first, learned scoring second, rules last. Result: index-to-index matching built from scratch, and the U.S. full-index run cut from 27 days to 6.
